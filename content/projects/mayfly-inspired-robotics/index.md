## Overview

During June and July 2025, I completed a two-month internship in a
bio-inspired robotics research environment at VISTEC Robotics Lab.

During the internship, I gained exposure to a robotics project inspired by
mayflies and learned more about how robotics research is approached in a
laboratory environment.

## What I Learned

The experience introduced me to areas including:

- Bio-inspired robotics
- Robotic mechanisms
- Engineering experimentation
- Research workflows
- Technical documentation
- Research reporting

This project represents my first significant exposure to robotics research
and helped motivate me to continue exploring robotics from both hardware and
software perspectives.

## Current Direction

Since the internship, I have been working on strengthening the foundations
that will help me participate more deeply in future robotics research,
including C/C++, Python, mathematics, circuits, embedded systems, and
robotics-related tools.

This repository documents my work and learning from the project rather than
claiming that I independently designed or developed the complete robotic
system.
